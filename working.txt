CONTENT PROBLEM (NOT FIXED)
=====================================================================================
The 6 "curated" research articles shown under each scan are not real publications. Every
PubMed ID, PMC ID and DOI was checked against PubMed, PubMed Central and Crossref: none matches
the article shown. They point to unrelated papers or to nothing. Examples:
  - "Efficacy of Multimodal Scans ... in Lobar Pneumonia Diagnosis" (shown with the chest X-ray):
    its "View Publication" link opens a study on glaucoma knowledge in Taiwan (PMC10928472).
  - "Differential Diagnosis of Pulmonary Consolidation and Effusion": PMID 38472911 is a study
    of pig carcass and meat quality; the DOI does not exist.
  - "Diffusion-Weighted MRI vs Non-Contrast CT in Early Stroke Triage": PMID 39019284 is a review
    of soil carbon in urban green spaces.
The 6 regional news items are also invented, and some are credited to real organisations
("Queensland Health Newsroom", "Australian Journal of Rural Health").
Recommendation: replace them with verified references (I can find real, relevant PubMed
articles for each scan for you to approve), or label them clearly as illustrative examples.
Data file: frontend/src/lib/medicalData.ts

BEFORE GOING LIVE
=====================================================================================
1. Create a Postgres database with pgvector (e.g. Neon), put its address in DATABASE_URL, and
   run: npm run db:migrate
2. In Vercel, set DATABASE_URL, GEMINI_API_KEY, IMGBB_API_KEY, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY,
   STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY and FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID (see frontend/.env.example).
   Switch the Stripe keys from test to live when you are ready to take real payments.
